# The blaster and the game

[Italiano](FUCILE.md)

The blaster is designed in the [PDF template](cardboard-blaster-template.pdf), which also has the assembly steps, and you can look at it in the [3D model](fucile-3d.html). This page only covers what matters to the software.

- **Phone sideways**, screen facing the player and the back camera free, facing the room. It can go either way round: the game notices by itself.
- **Trigger**: a lever with copper tape (or foil) and sponge that touches the screen in the middle of the bottom edge, in an area of about 25 x 20 mm. Any touch fires, but the trigger test checks that the pad lands right there.
- **Covered areas**: two low lips cover 6-10 mm of the bottom edge on the sides, and the lid covers 4-10 mm of the top edge. The game puts nothing important there.
- **First game**: in **Set up your blaster** run the trigger test, then calibrate the crosshair with the phone already in the blaster. Do it again for every phone.
- **Safety**: always keep the phone in its case, strap on the wrist and never around the neck. Before playing, gently shake the blaster upside down over the sofa: the phone must not move.
