# Materials for 3 blasters

[Italiano](MATERIALI.md)

## Cardboard and paper

- Corrugated cardboard, 3-4 mm: 3 or 4 medium boxes. A4 sheets are too small: the side piece is 38 cm long.
- Plain white matte A4 paper: about 15 sheets (template, 2 markers per player, 8 wall markers).
- Stiff A4 card: 6 sheets, to stiffen the markers you wear.

## Trigger

- Adhesive copper tape (at least 10 mm wide) or kitchen foil.
- Kitchen sponge, soft side only.
- Wooden skewers (pivot and 2 for the cheeks), toothpicks, small silicone hair ties (1 per blaster plus spares), 2 large office rubber bands (60-80 mm across) per blaster.
- Soft sponge about 1 cm thick: two strips per blaster (55 x 50 and 150 x 10 mm).

## Glue and tape

- PVA glue, a hot glue gun with sticks (an adult uses it).
- Masking tape and clear sticky tape.

## Safety

- Wrist strap, tied through the hole in the grip.
- A case on every phone. The box holds phones up to 175 x 82 x 13 mm with the case on.

## Wearing the markers

- Bibs with a cord, or safety pins. Markers on chest and back, nice and flat.

## Tools (an adult uses them)

- Craft knife with a new blade, cutting mat, metal ruler, pencil, scissors, clothes pegs.

## Before cutting

- Print page 1 of the PDF and check that the square measures 50 mm.
- Build one complete blaster with the biggest phone first, then the others.
